Featured Platform

TeacherSearch

AI-assisted recruitment built for education

TeacherSearch connects schools with teachers through a focused, two-sided recruitment platform. It combines transparent eligibility filters with privacy-conscious AI support to reduce repetitive screening work while keeping people in control of every hiring decision.

Schools receive an automatically generated shortlist after a role is activated. Teachers maintain their own profiles, choose when they are open to opportunities, and decide whether to apply after receiving an invitation.

Visit TeacherSearch

How matching works

  1. Check role, subject, and contract eligibility.
  2. Apply each teacher's travel radius or relocation preference.
  3. Screen an anonymised competency profile against the role and school context.
  4. Present suitable candidates for the school to review and invite.

Privacy-conscious summaries

AI creates concise competency summaries with personal identifiers removed, helping schools review relevant experience before identities are disclosed.

Geographic eligibility

Matching respects each candidate's stated travel radius and willingness to relocate, using real driving distance where required.

Automated shortlisting

An AI suitability screen works after deterministic eligibility checks to surface candidates whose experience fits the role.

Human-led decisions

TeacherSearch supports recruitment rather than replacing it: schools choose whom to invite, and teachers choose whether to submit a full application.
