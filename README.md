## About Oxford Advanced Learning

Oxford Advanced Learning (OAL) is a company initiative dedicated to providing premium services for the educational sector. These services include consultancy for university applications, private tutoring with a team of subject specialists, and software products that help teachers and schools in the recruitment process (TeacherSearch) and in advanced digital learning (IRMaths). OAL's software products are at the frontier of modern pedagogy, incorporating advanced AI capabilities.

Oxford Advanced Learning works in close partnership with IREducation Ltd., an education and learning-technology company based in Hong Kong, operating in China and the UK. IREducation Ltd. is the operating company; IREducation Inc. currently remains the publisher of record for IREducation's books. Together, we are building a complete ecosystem that redefines the way students, teachers, and institutions engage with education.

## Featured platform: TeacherSearch

[TeacherSearch](https://teachersearch.uk) is OAL and IREducation's AI-assisted recruitment platform for schools and teachers. Its live matching workflow combines subject and contract eligibility, candidate travel preferences, geographic filtering, anonymised competency summaries, and AI suitability screening to produce a shortlist for human review. Schools decide whom to invite, and teachers decide whether to submit a full application.

With a strong focus on Mathematics, Sciences, and university preparation, our long-term vision is to become the leading educational corporation in the UK — an all-encompassing "educational emporium" offering tools for students, teachers, and institutions.
